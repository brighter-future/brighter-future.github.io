What's new

Recent improvements. You are using version 0.25.2.

  1. November 24, 2026 · v0.25.2

    Small tidy-ups

    Latest

    The Lessons tile on the home screen now says what the app does for teachers and for the principal.

  2. November 24, 2026 · v0.25.1

    Lessons: tidier on a desktop screen

    The written working is now larger on big screens, and the Lesson builder buttons sit in neat rows.

    • The whiteboard writing grows with the player, so it is easy to read on a laptop or projector.
    • Fractions and integrals are no longer cut off at the bottom.
  3. November 24, 2026 · v0.25.0

    Lessons: the whole Form 6 Mathematics syllabus as the example

    The example course is now the full Form 6 Mathematics syllabus: 8 topics, 74 lessons and 233 syllabus points, added the next time Lessons opens.

    • Three Algebra lessons are published for students: linear equations (1.1), simultaneous equations (1.2, the finished lesson with four worked examples) and quadratic equations (1.6).
    • Teachers: eleven more lessons across the other topics are drafts with their steps and English words in place. Add your voice and the Kiribati, check them in Review, then publish.
    • Teachers: every other lesson is an empty draft with its syllabus points listed, ready for your own worked examples.
    • Steps you have already added Kiribati or a recording to are kept as they are. Your own lessons are not changed.
    • "Try the sample" on the import page now loads this same syllabus file.
  4. November 23, 2026 · v0.24.0

    See the step while you record

    For teachers

    In the Lesson builder, the recording screen now shows what the student will see, so a teacher can time the voice to it.

    • Video step: the clip plays without sound above your words as soon as recording starts, from the trimmed start to the trimmed end.
    • A bar under the clip shows how far it has played and how much is left. "Watch again" restarts it without stopping the recording.
    • Written working: the new line writes itself onto the board as you start to speak.
    • Picture: the picture is shown with the point you are talking about marked.
  5. November 22, 2026 · v0.23.0

    New dropdowns, faster clean-up, and lesson files that ask where to go

    Every dropdown in the app is now the same custom list, teachers get one page to delete in bulk, and importing a lesson file asks for the subject and class.

    • All dropdowns (choosing who you are, a class, a student, a subject, a line) open as one tidy list that fits the phone, with search on long lists.
    • Teachers: "Manage and delete" in the Lesson builder shows every subject and class. Tick topics to delete them together, delete a whole class, remove the examples, or delete everything in Lessons.
    • Teachers: each class there has an Export button, to keep a copy before deleting.
    • Teachers: "Import lesson file" now asks which subject and class it goes into. Sent to a different group, it becomes a separate copy.
  6. November 22, 2026 · v0.22.0

    Lessons: choose the group when you import

    For teachers

    When a teacher imports a syllabus, the app now asks where it goes: which subject and which class or level.

    • Step 2 of the import shows the subject and class from the file. Change them, pick a group that is already on the phone, or type a new one.
    • A file with no subject or level works too: you name the group in step 2.
    • Nothing can be imported or created without a subject and a class or level.
  7. November 22, 2026 · v0.21.0

    Lessons: subject, then class, and a way to tidy up

    Lessons always opens on the list of subjects, each with its classes. Teachers can now delete what they do not need.

    • Every phone shows Subject, then Class (Form 5, Form 6…), then topics. The example lessons sit under Mathematics, Form 6 and are marked "Example lessons".
    • Each page shows its subject and class in the breadcrumbs.
    • Teachers: delete a lesson, a topic, or a whole subject and class from the builder. The app asks once more first.
    • Teachers: "Remove the example lessons" takes out the examples and leaves your own outline.
  8. November 22, 2026 · v0.20.0

    Lessons: grouped by subject

    With more than one subject on a phone, Lessons now opens on a list of subjects instead of one long page of topics.

    • Each subject (Mathematics, Biology, Geography…) is a card with its classes. Tap a class to see only its topics.
    • "All subjects" and the breadcrumbs take you back.
    • Teachers: an import goes into the subject and class named in the file. The import page now shows which group that is, and whether it is new.
    • Teachers: the builder lists subjects and classes in one grouped box.
  9. November 22, 2026 · v0.19.4

  15. November 22, 2026 · v0.17.0

    Lessons: ready-made examples

    For teachers

    • They show written working with highlights, crossed-out terms and notes, a graph with numbered points, and a step that waits for Next.
    • Two lessons are published for students to play and one is left as a draft. The words are in English: add your own voice and the Kiribati.
    • Your own lessons are never changed or published by adding the examples.

  16. November 22, 2026 · v0.16.0

    Lessons: create your own outline

    For teachers

    • Add a topic or a lesson of your own to a syllabus you imported. Nothing that is already there changes.
    • The syllabus template is now on the builder page too: download it, fill it in a spreadsheet, then import it.
  17. November 22, 2026 · v0.15.0

    New app: Lessons

    A new app for your teacher's own lessons, step by step, in English and Kiribati.

    • Students: find lessons by topic, play them step by step, and switch between English and Kiribati at any time.
    • The player works like a video: a bar shows where you are, with a mark at each step. Tap or drag it to jump.
    • Save a lesson or a whole topic on your phone. You see the size first, and it plays with no internet.
    • Teachers: upload the syllabus as a CSV file and the app builds the outline of topics, lessons and sections.
    • Teachers: fill a lesson with worked examples made of picture steps (with numbered points), video clips and written working that writes itself.
    • Teachers: record your voice in English and Kiribati for each step, with Undo and Redo and a live preview.
    • Teachers: review a lesson, see what is missing, then publish it. Drafts never show to students.
    • Teachers: export a lesson or the whole course as one file and import it on another device.
  18. November 21, 2026 · v0.14.0

    You choose when to update

    The app no longer updates by itself. A small message tells you a new version is ready, and you decide.

    • "Save my place and update" keeps your lesson step, tab and typed answers, then installs the new version.
    • "Skip for now" closes the message. A small "Update available" button stays on screen so you can update later.
    • If you are recording, the app asks you to finish the recording first so it is not lost.
  19. November 21, 2026 · v0.13.0

    The app now updates itself

    When a new version is ready, the app counts down 5 seconds and installs it. You do not need to tap anything.

    • Tap "Update now" to skip the wait, or "Later" to stop it until you open the app again.
    • It never updates while a lesson is playing, a recording is running or you are typing. It waits until you stop.
    • Your place is kept: the lesson step, the tab, the answers you typed and the quiz answers you chose come back after the update.
    • The app looks for a new version when it opens and every 30 minutes.
  20. November 21, 2026 · v0.12.0

    Always know where you are in the lesson

    The video now shows which part and step you are on, how many seconds are left in the step, and how much of the whole lesson is left.

    • Under the picture: "Part 2 of 4: Substitution, Step 3 of 9", a bar for the step and the time left in it.
    • Below that: one bar per part, sized by its length, with the time watched and the time left in the whole lesson.
    • When the video stops for you to try on paper, it says "Your turn". That waiting time is not counted in the total.
    • A "~" or "about" means the time is a guess from the words. It becomes exact when a teacher has recorded the step.
