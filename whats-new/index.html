What's new

Recent improvements. You are using version 0.9.3.

  1. November 21, 2026 · v0.9.3

    Progress bar under the video in the studio

    Latest For teachers

    The recording studio now has a bar right below the video. It fills as you speak, marks the step you are on and shows in green the steps that already have your voice.

  2. November 21, 2026 · v0.9.2

    A timeline in the recording studio

    For teachers

    While you record, the studio shows where you are in the video: the play time, the total length, and a bar for each part of the lesson.

  3. November 21, 2026 · v0.9.1

    See the video while you record

    For teachers

    Recording now opens a studio that shows the video step on screen with the words to say, so you know what to say at each point.

    • Tap "Save, next step" when you finish a step. The video moves on and the recording carries on without stopping.
    • Made a mistake? "Start this step again" redoes only that step.
    • Works the same for English and Kiribati. If a step has no translation yet, the English words are shown as a guide.
    • Use the arrows to move through the video and "Listen" to hear what you recorded.
  4. November 21, 2026 · v0.9.0

    Record your own voice for the video lesson

    For teachers

    Teachers can now replace the words and the voice of the Simultaneous equations video, step by step, in English and in Kiribati.

    • Open the lesson in Math Lab as a teacher and tap "Edit and record".
    • Each step shows what is on screen, the words to say, a box for the translation and a record button for each language.
    • Students hear your recording in place of the computer voice, and can switch between English and Kiribati.
    • Steps you have not recorded yet still play with the computer voice.
    • The separate Lessons app is gone: recording now lives inside the lesson itself.
  5. November 21, 2026 · v0.8.2

    Lessons is now a teacher tool

    For teachers

    Only teachers see the Lessons app. Teachers can still preview each lesson the way a student would see it.

  6. November 21, 2026 · v0.8.1

    Update message is easier to read

    The "new version is ready" strip at the top is now clear to read in both light and dark mode.

  7. November 21, 2026 · v0.8.0

    Easier to find your way around

    Every screen now has the same layout, so you always know where you are and how to get back.

    • A menu of your apps is always on screen: at the bottom on a phone, at the top on a computer. The app you are in is marked.
    • Every page starts with a trail (Home › App › Page), a clear title and one line that says what the page is for.
    • The top bar always shows who you are viewing as: Student, Teacher or Principal, each with its own colour.
    • Home lists only the apps for your role.
    • Math Lab and the home page have a calmer, more professional look. The colours stay.
  8. November 21, 2026 · v0.7.0

    Lessons in two languages, in the teacher's own voice

    For teachers and students

    A new Lessons app: teachers write each section in English and Kiribati and record their voice. Students choose a language, read and listen.

    • Teachers: edit the English text and type the translation side by side.
    • Record, play, re-record or delete a voice recording for each section and each language. The text shows large while you record.
    • Each section shows what is ready: text and audio for both languages.
    • Students: switch between English and Kiribati at the top. If a translation or recording is missing, English is used and a note says so.
    • Export a lesson with its recordings as one file and import it on another device.
    • Starts with the Form 6 lesson on simultaneous equations, in seven sections. Everything works offline.
  9. November 21, 2026 · v0.6.1

    Form 6 lesson moved to the top of Math Lab

    For students

    The simultaneous equations lesson is now the first thing you see in Math Lab, above the topic tiles.

  10. November 21, 2026 · v0.6.0

    First Form 6 lesson: simultaneous equations

    For students

    Math Lab now has a full PSSC lesson with a narrated video, the written lesson, guided steps and practice.

    • Watch: a voice reads the lesson while the board writes each step. It stops at "pause and try this yourself".
    • Read: the complete written lesson, with every algebra step shown. It can be printed.
    • Try: tap points on a graph to find where two lines cross, then solve step by step with instant feedback.
    • Practice: 8 questions from easy to exam style, with hints and worked solutions.
    • Works offline. The voice is the one built into the phone, so nothing is downloaded.
  11. November 20, 2026 · v0.5.0

    Made for computers too

    On a laptop or desktop the app now uses the whole screen instead of a narrow phone column.

    • Dashboard shows charts and tables side by side.
    • Quiz results show the hardest questions next to the score list.
    • Library books show chapters beside the cover; attendance and at-risk lists use two columns.
    • Phones look the same as before.
  12. November 20, 2026 · v0.4.1

    Fixing an answer key re-marks the quiz

    For teachers

    If a teacher corrects a quiz after students have taken it, their scores are now marked again automatically.

    • Works for quizzes taken on a device and for scanned paper sheets.
  13. November 20, 2026 · v0.4.0

    Teacher Quizzes, a calmer look and update notices

    For teachers

    Quizzes can now be made, taken and graded, including paper bubble sheets graded from a phone photo.

    • Teachers create multiple-choice quizzes and assign them to a class.
    • Students take quizzes on a device and see their score and the correct answers straight away.
    • Printable bubble sheets, graded on the phone with the camera. No internet needed.
    • Results for each quiz: class average, score per student and the hardest questions.
    • The home screen only shows the apps that belong to your role.
    • A strip at the top tells you when a new version is ready. Tap it to update.
    • Simpler, squarer design and loading placeholders while pages open.
    • This "What's new" page.
  14. November 20, 2026 · v0.3.0

    Install card

    The home screen now explains how to install the app on Android, iPhone and desktop.

    • An Install button appears when the browser allows it.
    • New app icon for the home screen.
  15. November 20, 2026 · v0.2.0

    Moroni High Learning Hub

    The math demo became one offline app that holds a library, a dashboard and the Math Lab.

    • Student Library: download textbook chapters and read them with no internet.
    • Principal Dashboard: attendance register, trends, at-risk students, CSV and printable report.
    • Role switcher for student, teacher and principal. No login needed for the demo.
    • Works offline after the first visit. Changes are queued for a future server.
  16. November 20, 2026 · v0.1.1

    More math topics and Grade 10

    Math Lab grew from line graphs to four topics at two levels.

    • Ratio and scale, area and algebra.
    • Sliders, a balance-scale equation solver and live shapes.
  17. November 19, 2026 · v0.1.0

    First version

    Interactive line-graph lessons for Grades 6 and 7.
