A ratio compares two amounts

A smoothie recipe uses 2 cups of juice for every 3 cups of water. We write that as 2 : 3.

Make a double batch and you get 4 : 6. The drink tastes exactly the same because the ratio did not change.

2 : 3 = 4 : 6 = 6 : 9
